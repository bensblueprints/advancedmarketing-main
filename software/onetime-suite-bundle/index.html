No — the bundle is a license to the whole catalog. Install what you need now and grab the rest later, whenever you need it, at no extra cost.

What happens when new apps ship?

They join the bundle automatically. The One-Time Suite adds new apps regularly, and bundle owners get every one of them included, forever, with no upsell.

Is this really cheaper than buying a few apps individually?

Honestly — if you only need 2-3 apps, buying those individually may cost less than $997. The bundle earns its price once you'd want more than a handful, or want the option to grab any future app for free.
