How to Capture More Leads Without a Typeform Subscription

By Advanced Marketing · 2026-07-27 · Filed under do it without the subscription

You do not need a Typeform subscription to capture more leads. Typeform would like you to believe the job and the subscription are the same thing — that is how $29/mo becomes a permanent fixture of your budget. It is not. The job is the job; the subscription is just the toll booth someone parked in front of it.

Here is how to capture more leads with Formforge instead — a one-time $39 self-hosted web app that does the work on hardware you control. Self-hosted form builder + submissions inbox. No response caps.

Step by step: Capture More Leads

  1. Buy once on Whop. One-time $39 for the packaged one-click installer, plus updates and setup support.
  2. Build your form. Drag fields into order, pick single-page or one-question-per-screen, set your accent color.
  3. Share or embed. Send /f/your-slug or copy the iframe snippet. Responses land in your inbox — unlimited, exportable to CSV.

The toolkit

Build beautiful one-question-per-screen forms (or classic single-page forms), share a link or embed them anywhere, and collect unlimited responses into your own inbox — on your own server, with your own database. Typeform wants $348/yr and caps your responses.

  • Drag-orderable field editor — 10 field types: short/long text, email, number, dropdown, radio, checkboxes, date, file upload, star rating.
  • Two display modes — One-question-per-screen "Typeform-style" flow with keyboard navigation, or classic single page.
  • Form settings — Title, description, button text, success message or redirect URL, accent color, open/closed toggle.
  • Submissions inbox — Table view, detail drawer, one-click CSV export, downloadable file answers.
  • File uploads — Stored locally with size and type limits. Respondent files never touch a third party.
  • Spam protection — Honeypot field + per-IP rate limiting, client and server validation.
  • Email notifications — Bring your own SMTP, get an email per submission.
  • 100% local — SQLite database, local file storage, zero telemetry, zero external calls.

Common questions

Is it really free on GitHub?
Yes — MIT source at github.com/bensblueprints/form-builder. $39 buys the packaged installer, 1-click setup, updates and setup support.

Does it have logic jumps?
Not yet — fields are linear. Typeform's conditional logic and calculator are genuinely more advanced; if your forms branch heavily, Typeform still earns its price.

Are responses really unlimited?
Yes — they're rows in your own SQLite file. The only limit is your disk.
