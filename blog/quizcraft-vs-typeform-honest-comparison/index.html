Quizcraft vs Typeform: Honest Comparison for Agencies

By Advanced Marketing · 2026-07-27 · Filed under honest comparisons

Quizcraft vs Typeform is not really a feature fight — it is a pricing-philosophy fight. Typeform rents you the tool at $25+/mo and stops working when you stop paying. Quizcraft sells you the tool for $79, once, and keeps working forever. Both are legitimate models; which one wins depends on how your agency actually works.

So here is the honest version: a side-by-side table from the real specs, the three-year math, and a straight answer on who should stay on Typeform. We sell Quizcraft, so read our verdict with that in mind — but the numbers below are checkable.

Where Quizcraft wins

Typeform starts at $25 a month and meters your responses; Outgrow starts at $14 and scales up fast with volume. That's $300 a year and climbing to run a quiz. Quizcraft is $79 once, with unlimited quizzes and unlimited responses, because it runs on your server.

  • Price: $79 once — vs $25+/mo for Typeform.
  • Responses: Unlimited — vs 100-10k/mo metered for Typeform.
  • Branching logic: Yes — vs yes for Typeform.

A lead-gen quiz that captures 500 emails a month climbs into Typeform's $83 tier — Quizcraft is $79, once.

Where Typeform wins

Typeform has the incumbent's advantages: brand recognition with clients, a hosted cloud you never have to think about, and an ecosystem of integrations built over years. If your team needs zero maintenance, lives inside Typeform's collaboration features, or bills the subscription through to clients anyway, Typeform is a defensible choice. Quizcraft is self-hosted web app — you control the hardware, which also means updates and backups are yours to manage.

The verdict

For agencies that want to capture leads with quizzes without a permanent subscription line, Quizcraft is the better buy: same core job, $79 once, data on your own hardware. For teams deep in Typeform's ecosystem, stay — and revisit when the renewal stings enough.
