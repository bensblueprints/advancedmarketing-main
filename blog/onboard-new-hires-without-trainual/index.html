How to Onboard New Hires Without a Trainual Subscription

By Advanced Marketing · 2026-07-27 · Filed under do it without the subscription

You do not need a Trainual subscription to onboard new hires. Trainual would like you to believe the job and the subscription are the same thing — that is how $99/mo becomes a permanent fixture of your budget. It is not. The job is the job; the subscription is just the toll booth someone parked in front of it.

Here is how to onboard new hires with Rampcheck instead — a one-time $79 self-hosted web app that does the work on hardware you control. New-hire onboarding you own forever.

Step by step: Onboard New Hires

  1. Buy once on Whop. One payment of $79 gets you the packaged Windows installer with updates, or clone the MIT source at github.com/bensblueprints/onboarding-checklist-mvp and run it free.
  2. Run desktop or deploy. Run npm run desktop as a local window, or docker compose onto a $5 VPS when you need it public.
  3. Assign a checklist. Pick a role template, assign it to a hire, and send them their private portal link.

The toolkit

Trainual is $99/mo — over $1,188 a year, forever, and more than $3,500 by year three. Rampcheck is the core of it self-hosted: role-based checklists, an SOP library, a private portal for every new hire, quizzes and a manager dashboard. $79, once — still $79 in year three.

  • Checklist templates per role — Multi-step onboarding across day 1 / week 1 / month 1, each step with a due-day offset, assignable to a hire in one click.
  • New-hire portal — Every hire gets a private tokenized link with their checklist, progress bar, linked docs and self check-off — no account needed.
  • Content library — SOPs and policies as rich HTML docs with optional video links, organized by category and linked from checklist steps.
  • Quiz steps — Multiple-choice checks with a pass threshold; the step only completes on a passing score, and answers never leak to the portal payload.
  • Manager dashboard — All active onboardings, overdue-step alerts, completion-rate reporting and per-step manager verification.
