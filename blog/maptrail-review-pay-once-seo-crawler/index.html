Maptrail Review: the Pay-Once SEO Crawler Tool for Marketers

By Advanced Marketing · 2026-07-27 · Filed under pay-once tool reviews

Maptrail is a $19, pay-once SEO crawler tool that positions itself against Screaming Frog (£199/yr (~$250/yr)). The short version, after running it through real client work: for most marketing teams it pays for itself almost immediately. Pays for itself in under a month of a Screaming Frog license — and every year after is pure savings.

But "replaces Screaming Frog" deserves an honest look rather than a cheer. Full disclosure up front: we distribute Maptrail through OneTimeSuite, so factor that in. Here is what it does well, where it falls short, and who should keep paying Screaming Frog.

What you get for $19

Crawl any site on your own machine and ship a clean sitemap. Local website crawler and sitemap generator — XML, HTML and a full CSV SEO report, offline.

  • Local breadth-first crawler — Enter a URL and crawl the whole site from your machine with a live progress table — status, depth, title, meta length, H1, word count.
  • Depth, limit & pattern controls — Set max depth and page limit, plus include/exclude URL patterns (substrings or /regex/) to skip tag and sort pages.
  • Standards-compliant sitemap.xml — lastmod pulled from real Last-Modified headers, priority scaled by crawl depth.
  • Automatic 50k index chunking — Sites over 50,000 URLs split into numbered sitemaps plus a sitemap-index.xml, exactly per the sitemaps.org spec.
  • Full CSV SEO report — Every crawled page with status, title, meta, H1, word count, canonical and noindex — including the 404s the sitemap correctly leaves out, so you can fix them.
  • Local crawl history — Every crawl saved locally so you can reload, re-export or delete it — nothing leaves your disk.

The verdict

Who Maptrail is for

Agencies and marketers who crawl client sites for SEO regularly and are tired of renting the capability. If the tool's job shows up in your week more than once or twice — client deliverables, campaign assets, reporting — the $19 one-time price is a rounding error against $250 a year for Screaming Frog. It is also the right pick if client data privacy matters to you: everything stays on your own hardware.

Who should stay on Screaming Frog

Teams whose workflow is genuinely built on Screaming Frog's cloud — shared workspaces, its specific integrations, clients who log in there directly. If switching would cost you more in retraining and process change than $250 a year, stay put. And if you only crawl a website once a quarter, a free tier somewhere may be all you need; buy Maptrail when the job becomes routine.

Score it plainly: capability for the core job, 9/10. Convenience versus a hosted incumbent, 7/10. Value for money, 10/10 — $19 once against £199/yr (~$250/yr) is not close.

Common questions

Is it really free on GitHub?
Yes — Maptrail is MIT-licensed at github.com/bensblueprints/sitemap-generator-mvp. The $19 buys the signed Windows installer, 1-click setup and updates; building from source is free.

Does it do everything Screaming Frog does?
No, and we'd rather say so. Screaming Frog also does log-file analysis, JS rendering and integrations. If you need all that, buy it. If you need clean sitemaps and a crawl report, Maptrail does that for $19 once.

Does my crawl data get uploaded?
Never. Maptrail makes network requests only to the site you tell it to crawl — no telemetry, no update pings, no account. History lives in a local SQLite file.
