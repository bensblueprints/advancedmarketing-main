🎥 ClipDeck · replaces Loom

How Agencies Use ClipDeck to Record Client Walkthroughs

By Advanced Marketing · 2026-07-27 · Filed under agency use cases

Ask an account manager what actually eats the week and the answer is rarely strategy — it is production. Screen + mic recording with local library, MP4 & two-pass GIF export. No cloud. That single job, repeated across every client account, is where agency hours go to die. It is also where a monthly tool like Loom quietly becomes a permanent line item at $15/mo.

This is the workflow agencies run with ClipDeck to record client walkthroughs — a $29, pay-once desktop app that does the job on hardware you control, with no subscription attached.

The ClipDeck workflow, step by step

The whole point is that there is no ceremony. Three steps and the job is done:

  1. Buy once on Whop. One-time $29 for the packaged installer, free updates forever.
  2. Pick a screen or window. Live-preview source picker, mic toggle, hit record. A floating pill keeps the timer out of your shot.
  3. Trim & export. MP4 for sharing anywhere, or a properly-paletted GIF for docs and PRs. Files stay in your Videos folder.

The features that matter in client work

Record your screen and mic, save everything locally, export MP4 or GIF — no cloud, no monthly bill, no watermark, no upload limits. Your recordings live on your disk, not on someone else's server.

  • Source picker with live previews — Record any screen or individual window — thumbnails included.
  • Microphone audio — One toggle, mixed cleanly with system audio into a single track.
  • Floating recording pill — Tiny always-on-top timer with a stop button, so the main window stays out of the shot.
  • Local library — Every recording auto-saves to Videos/Screen Recorder with thumbnails, and plays right in the app.
  • MP4 export — H.264 + faststart — plays everywhere, with live progress.
  • Optimized GIF export — Proper two-pass palette generation, not the muddy single-pass junk.
  • Trim — Set start/end before exporting — no separate editor needed.
  • 100% local — Zero telemetry, zero network calls, zero accounts, zero watermarks.

Common questions

Is it really free on GitHub?
Yes — MIT source at github.com/bensblueprints/screen-recorder-desktop. $29 buys the signed installer, 1-click setup and updates.

Does it upload my videos anywhere?
No. There is no cloud. Recordings save to your Videos folder and never leave it unless you share them yourself.

Can I share a link like Loom?
Not built in — that's the honest trade-off. Loom's instant share-link is its killer feature. ClipDeck gives you the file; share it via Drive, Slack or your own hosting.
