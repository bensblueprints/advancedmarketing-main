Vaultly vs 1Password: Honest Comparison for Agencies

By Advanced Marketing · 2026-07-27 · Filed under honest comparisons

Vaultly vs 1Password Teams is not really a feature fight — it is a pricing-philosophy fight. 1Password Teams rents you the tool at $7.99/user/mo and stops working when you stop paying. Vaultly sells you the tool for $99, once, and keeps working forever. Both are legitimate models; which one wins depends on how your agency actually works.

So here is the honest version: a side-by-side table from the real specs, the three-year math, and a straight answer on who should stay on 1Password Teams. We sell Vaultly, so read our verdict with that in mind — but the numbers below are checkable.

Where Vaultly wins

1Password Teams is $7.99 per user per month, so a five-person team pays roughly $1,438 over three years, forever, with everything in someone else's cloud. Vaultly runs the same zero-knowledge model on your own $5 VPS: everything is encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM before it reaches the server, which only ever stores ciphertext. $99, once, however many people you add.

  • Price: $99 once — vs $7.99/user/mo for 1Password Teams.
  • Zero-knowledge encryption: Yes — vs yes for 1Password Teams.
  • Your data on your server: Yes — vs no for 1Password Teams.

Five months of 1Password for a five-person team already costs more than Vaultly, which then never charges you a per-seat cent again.

Where 1Password Teams wins

1Password Teams has the incumbent's advantages: brand recognition with clients, a hosted cloud you never have to think about, and an ecosystem of integrations built over years. If your team needs zero maintenance, lives inside 1Password Teams's collaboration features, or bills the subscription through to clients anyway, 1Password Teams is a defensible choice. Vaultly is self-hosted web app — you control the hardware, which also means updates and backups are yours to manage.

The verdict

For agencies that want to manage team passwords without a permanent subscription line, Vaultly is the better buy: same core job, $99 once, data on your own hardware. For teams deep in 1Password Teams's ecosystem, stay — and revisit when the renewal stings enough.

Common questions

Is it really free on GitHub?
Yes. Vaultly is MIT-licensed at github.com/bensblueprints/password-manager-selfhosted-mvp and always will be. The $99 buys the packaged 1-click installer and setup; building from source is free.

Where does my data live, and what can the server see?
On your own server, as ciphertext. The server can see email addresses, item counts and timestamps, vault names and audit metadata, but not names, URLs, usernames, passwords or notes, which stay inside the encryption.

What if I forget my master password?
It is unrecoverable by design; there is no reset. Export regularly (the export is ciphertext too) and store it safely.
