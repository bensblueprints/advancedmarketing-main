How to Run NPS Surveys Without a Delighted Subscription

By Advanced Marketing · 2026-07-27 · Filed under do it without the subscription

You do not need a Delighted subscription to run NPS surveys. Delighted would like you to believe the job and the subscription are the same thing — that is how $224+/mo becomes a permanent fixture of your budget. It is not. The job is the job; the subscription is just the toll booth someone parked in front of it.

Here is how to run NPS surveys with Pulsecheck instead — a one-time $79 self-hosted web app that does the work on hardware you control. NPS, CSAT and CES surveys you own forever.

Step by step: Run NPS Surveys

  1. Buy once on Whop. One payment of $79 gets the packaged Windows installer, or clone the MIT source at github.com/bensblueprints/nps-survey-tool-mvp and run it yourself for free.
  2. Deploy or run desktop. Docker-compose onto a $5 VPS to collect responses publicly, or run the desktop build locally.
  3. Send or embed the survey. Share the standalone link, send via your SMTP, or drop the widget script on your site, then watch scores and detractor alerts roll in.

The toolkit

Delighted charges $224 a month to ask your customers one question and store the answers in their cloud. Pulsecheck does the same NPS, CSAT and CES capture on your own server, with detractor alerts and per-segment breakdowns. $79, once. It is subtraction.

  • Three survey types — NPS (0-10), CSAT (1-5) and CES (1-7), each with correct documented score math (NPS = %promoters minus %detractors, ranged -100 to 100).
  • Three delivery channels — A standalone survey link, personalized email sends via your own SMTP, and an embeddable widget script.
  • One response per recipient — Email-keyed dedupe at the database level means a second submit gets a friendly already-responded message.
  • Detractor alerts — A Slack-ready webhook and/or email fire instantly on low scores, and soft-fail so they never block response capture.
  • Dashboard — Score trend over time, the promoter/passive/detractor split, and a response feed with comments.
  • Segments — Pass tags like plan:pro with responses and get per-segment NPS breakdowns.
  • XSS-safe by construction — The public survey page escapes every user-controlled string and the widget renders via textContent only.
