🔗 Trimly · replaces Bitly

How Agencies Use Trimly to Track Campaign Links

By Advanced Marketing · 2026-07-27 · Filed under agency use cases

Ask an account manager what actually eats the week and the answer is rarely strategy — it is production. Branded short links, QR codes and click analytics — on your domain, in your SQLite file. That single job, repeated across every client account, is where agency hours go to die. It is also where a monthly tool like Bitly quietly becomes a permanent line item at $29/mo.

This is the workflow agencies run with Trimly to track campaign links — a $29, pay-once self-hosted web app that does the job on hardware you control, with no subscription attached.

The Trimly workflow, step by step

The whole point is that there is no ceremony. Three steps and the job is done:

  1. Buy once on Whop. One-time $29 — installer, updates and setup support included.
  2. Point your domain. Deploy to a $5 VPS with Docker, point go.yourbrand.com at it, put free Cloudflare in front for country analytics.
  3. Create links, watch clicks. Custom slugs, UTM builder, QR codes, and a full analytics dashboard you own.

The features that matter in client work

Bitly charges $348/year just to use your own domain with your links — and your click data lives on their servers. Trimly runs on your $5 VPS (or right on your desktop), keeps every click in a local SQLite file you own, and never phones home.

  • Branded short links — Auto-generated or custom slugs on your own domain.
  • Click analytics — Total + unique clicks, clicks-over-time chart, top referrers, devices, browsers, countries.
  • QR code for every link — Downloadable PNG and SVG.
  • 301 / 302 per link — SEO-permanent or trackable-temporary — your call, editable any time.
  • UTM builder — Built into the create form — tag campaigns without a spreadsheet.
  • Bulk CSV import & export — Migrate off Bitly in one paste.
  • Country detection, zero paid APIs — Reads CF-IPCountry behind free Cloudflare; degrades gracefully otherwise.
  • 100% local & private — SQLite storage, no telemetry, no tracking pixels, no external calls.

How agencies put it to work

  • Client work: Branded short links — auto-generated or custom slugs on your own domain, and there is no meter running in the background while you work.
  • Campaign production: Click analytics — total + unique clicks, clicks-over-time chart, top referrers, devices, browsers, countries, which is margin you keep instead of handing to a SaaS vendor.
  • Reporting & handoff: QR code for every link — downloadable PNG and SVG, and there is no per-seat cost when the whole team piles in.
  • Retainer margins: 301 / 302 per link — SEO-permanent or trackable-temporary — your call, editable any time, which means one less shared login and one less quota to babysit.
  • Volume days: UTM builder — built into the create form — tag campaigns without a spreadsheet, so the tenth revision costs the same as the first — nothing.

What it costs vs Bitly

Bitly runs about $348 a year — $1044 over three years, per the current pricing. Trimly is $29, once. One month of Bitly's custom-domain plan costs the same as Trimly does once.

Common questions

Is it really free on GitHub?
Yes — MIT source at github.com/bensblueprints/link-shortener. The $29 buys the packaged 1-click installer, updates and setup support.

Do I need a server for public links?
Yes — public short links need a public host, so budget a $5/mo VPS. Desktop mode works fully offline for QR generation and campaign planning. Even VPS + Trimly year one costs less than 3 months of Bitly.

Can I migrate from Bitly?
Yes — bulk CSV import is built in. Export from Bitly, paste, done. Printed QR codes keep working because destinations stay editable.
