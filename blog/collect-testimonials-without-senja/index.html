How to Collect Testimonials Without a Senja Subscription

By Advanced Marketing · 2026-07-27 · Filed under do it without the subscription

You do not need a Senja subscription to collect testimonials. Senja would like you to believe the job and the subscription are the same thing — that is how $19/mo becomes a permanent fixture of your budget. It is not. The job is the job; the subscription is just the toll booth someone parked in front of it.

Here is how to collect testimonials with Bravowall instead — a one-time $29 self-hosted web app that does the work on hardware you control. Collect testimonials, moderate them, embed a Wall of Love anywhere — self-hosted.

Step by step: Collect Testimonials

  1. Buy once on Whop. One-time $29 for the packaged Windows installer plus priority support — no subscription, ever.
  2. Run it your way. Desktop app for zero-server use, or docker compose up on a $5 VPS when the collection form needs to be public.
  3. Collect, approve, embed. Share your /collect link, approve the good ones, and paste one script tag anywhere you want the wall.

The toolkit

Senja charges $19/month — $228 a year, forever — to display quotes your customers wrote for free. Bravowall covers the whole loop: hosted collection forms, a moderation queue, a wall-of-love embed that can't break your site, and PNG card exports for social. One-time $29, on your own box.

  • Collection forms — A hosted page per product at /collect/your-slug: name, company, photo upload, 1–5 stars, testimonial text, optional YouTube/Loom/Vimeo link, consent checkbox.
  • Moderation queue — Everything lands as pending. Approve, reject, feature, or fix typos before publishing — the original text is always kept.
  • Wall of Love embed — One script tag renders a masonry wall inside shadow DOM: your site's CSS can't break it, its styles never leak out. Theme, accent, max items via data attributes.
  • PNG card export — Turn any testimonial into a polished 1080×1080 or 1600×900 image card, rendered server-side with sharp — quote auto-sizing, stars, avatar, your accent color.
  • CSV import — Migrate from Senja or Testimonial.to exports in one upload, with per-row error reporting.
  • Snippets & counters — Copy-paste standalone HTML per testimonial, plus a count API for "loved by N customers" badges.
  • Spam protection — Honeypot field plus a 10-submissions-per-hour-per-IP rate limit on the public form.
  • 100% yours — SQLite on disk, avatars on disk, no telemetry, no external services. Desktop app or $5 VPS via Docker.

Common questions

Is it really free on GitHub?
Yes — the full source is MIT at github.com/bensblueprints/bravowall and always will be. The $29 buys the packaged installer, 1-click setup and updates instead of a terminal session.

Can visitors record video testimonials?
No — that's a deliberate non-goal, and it's Senja's genuine edge. Bravowall embeds video links (YouTube, Loom, Vimeo) instead of recording and hosting video itself. If in-form video recording is essential, Senja earns its fee there.

Will the embed break my site's styling?
It can't. The wall renders inside shadow DOM, so your CSS can't reach in and its styles can't leak out. Masonry layout is pure CSS columns — no layout JavaScript.
