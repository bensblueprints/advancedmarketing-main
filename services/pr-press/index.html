FAQ

Questions, Answered

Can you guarantee coverage in a specific publication?

No reputable PR firm can guarantee a specific outlet — editorial decisions belong to journalists. What we guarantee is a strong, persistent, professional outreach effort built on genuinely newsworthy angles.

How is this different from buying ads?

Ads are paid and stop the moment you stop paying. Earned media is credibility you can’t buy directly — it builds trust, SEO authority and brand recall that compound over time.

What kinds of businesses do you do PR for?

Founders, e-commerce brands, agencies and local businesses looking to build authority. On your call we’ll assess whether PR is the right lever for your stage.

How long until we see coverage?

PR is a longer game than ads. Initial placements often land within the first 1–3 months as relationships and pitches mature.
