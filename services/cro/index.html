FAQ

Questions, Answered

How much does conversion rate optimization cost?

CRO runs as a month-to-month program quoted after a free funnel audit — sized to your traffic volume and testing velocity. The audit itself is free, and even the first round of quick fixes often pays for the program.

Is CRO worth it for a small business?

If you’re paying for any traffic at all, yes — a lift from 2% to 3% conversion is a 50% revenue increase from the same spend. Lower-traffic sites still benefit from expert audits and best-practice fixes before formal A/B testing becomes viable.

Do I need a lot of traffic for CRO to work?

More traffic lets tests reach significance faster, but even lower-traffic sites benefit from expert audits and best-practice fixes. We’ll assess your situation on the call.

Does CRO work with my ad campaigns?

It supercharges them. Lifting conversion rate lowers your effective cost per acquisition, making every ad dollar go further — which is why we often pair CRO with paid media.

What do you actually test?

Headlines, offers, page layouts, calls-to-action, forms, checkout flow and trust elements — anything that influences whether a visitor takes action.
